## Overview

Primary surface: **Decide/Learn**. An iPhone puzzle visitor should understand the real mechanic, enjoy the characters and join the launch list in about two mobile screens. This overview is a standalone game microsite at the existing URL, not a portfolio/docs page. The personal portfolio, app directory, support and privacy retain their existing shells/addresses. No owner-name header, breadcrumbs, documentation tabs, personal-site chat or repeated attribution.

Reference strategy: blended original. The installed MIT [Figma template](https://github.com/VoltAgent/awesome-design-md) is a secondary design analysis, not official Figma guidance. The [Two Dots game page](https://www.twodots-game.com/en/game) is an official game-market reference. Borrow these principles without cloning either layout: (1) quiet navigation lets colorful product art lead; (2) one headline/one primary action; (3) real gameplay is the largest product evidence; (4) a recurring character family unifies illustration/motion; (5) mobile sections follow a simple linear reading order. Supporting inspiration: Jordan's vault Design/landing-page-and-hero-video-playbook.md and [saved character-motion reference](https://x.com/DesignByMoein/status/2096289484577071567).

## Colors

Use the native sage canvas, sunny yellow, dark ink and ivory paper. Leaf green, moon lilac and mailbox blue live in actual stamp art. No indigo gradients, glass or portfolio paper texture. Semantic error text remains high-contrast and visible. Keep type on quiet surfaces; generated scenery is decorative and must not reduce legibility.

## Typography

Use existing locally bundled Baloo2 700 for the game mark/headline and system sans for clear body/form text. Headline clamps from about44px mobile to72px desktop. Body16–18px. No eyebrow labels, all-caps technical captions, tiny badge explanations or extra marketing copy.

## Layout

Header: game wordmark only; no pause control. Hero: short headline and one-line premise beside a large real gameplay preview. Generated postcard landscape anchors the hero, never replaces genuine board UI. A short visual mechanic strip uses actual Stash/Flip/Send art without feature-card chrome. One launch form completes the page. Footer: Support, Privacy, Blum Studio. Necessary consent/removal/state copy remains; detailed game rules and purchases stay in support. Keep the whole page compact; no carousel, testimonials, fake metrics, pricing section or long lore.

## Elevation & Depth

Warm paper,2px ink borders, a3px tactile button base and sparse postal layering. A real licensed phone PNG may contain genuine UI; never generate/draw a fake iPhone. The generated world cannot contain invented gameplay, readable text or a phone.

## Shapes

Rounded rectangles and original stamp perforations. Use existing recolorable sun/leaf/moon SVGs and fun-isometric postal artwork; retain source/license provenance. No emoji substitutes or hand-built copies of native gameplay.

## Components

Submitting the clearly named notification form is the app-specific request; send consent:true only on valid submit. Retain the endpoint, version, scope, honeypot, no-cookie fetch, native validation, timeout, success/error states and no-JS/unconfigured fallback. Adapt its copy/theme without owner-name repetition. Buttons and links have44px targets, visible focus, hover/press/disabled/loading states. Keep a semantic h1, skip link and stable canonical. Availability remains Coming soon; never imply App Store approval/download availability.

## Do's and Don'ts

Generated ambiance and actual gameplay are separate assets. Motion loops silently by default without a pause control; offscreen/hidden-tab media pauses, and reduced motion/Save-Data use posters. Browser autoplay refusal leaves the poster. Reuse existing delivered/collection animations; no new generation. Posters must tell the same product story without video or JS. Lazy-load media only after poster/critical content; no new JS framework, animation package, 3D runtime or dependency. Use fun-isometric's existing authored motion/export family rather than inventing idle CSS wobbles. Generated animation should have a short coherent loop and stay below3MB where practical. Keep screenshot/clip provenance and budget receipts. Verify desktop/mobile, keyboard, reduced motion, no-JS, real form success/failure, and console before shipping.

October28 persona audit: preserve intentional postal outlines and real pile/route numbering; no decorative labels, repeated subtitles or marketing section numbers. The hero notification link focuses the email field after native anchor navigation. Public Support mirrors native mini/full-pack overlap and conditional analytics disclosures.
